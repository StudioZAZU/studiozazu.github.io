Deja Me Privacy Policy

Oct 11, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Deja Me (the "Game") handles information. The Game is developed and published by ZAZU Studios, based in South Africa ("we", "us").

By downloading or playing the Game, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number or any account details. The Game has no sign-up or login.

The Game saves the following only on your own device, so it works between sessions:

  • Your best score
  • Unlocked colours and trails, and your selected style
  • Your settings (sound, music, vibration, controls)
  • Whether you have seen the tutorial
  • How many games you have played (used to decide when to show ads)
  • Your sparks balance and upgrade levels
  • How many free-sparks ads you have watched today, and when (used for the daily limit)

This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, AdMob may automatically collect information from your device, including:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system and app version
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • Crash and performance information related to ads

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Google may use it to show you personalised or non-personalised ads, to measure ad performance and to prevent fraud. You can read how Google uses this information here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the Game's Settings screen.

Rewarded ads. Some ads are optional. You choose to watch them in exchange for an in-game reward, such as continuing a game or earning extra sparks.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • Ad personalisation: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).
  • Consent: players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland can update their ad consent in the Game's Settings screen.
  • Deleting your data: uninstalling the Game deletes all game data saved on your device.

If you are in South Africa, you have rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Users elsewhere may have similar rights under their local laws. Contact us to exercise them.

Security

We do not store your personal information on our own servers. Information collected by Google AdMob is handled under Google's security practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
