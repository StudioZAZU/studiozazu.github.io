Deja Me Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 Nov 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Deja Me (the "Game") handles information. The Game is developed and published by ZAZU Studios, based in South Africa ("we", "us").

By downloading or playing the Game, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address or phone number, and we do not run our own servers. The Game has no sign-up of its own. Signing in with Google Play Games is optional (see below), and the Game works fully without it.

Information saved on your device

The Game saves the following on your own device, so it works between sessions:

  • Your best score
  • Unlocked colours and trails, and your selected style
  • Your settings (sound, music, vibration, controls)
  • Whether you have seen the tutorial
  • How many games you have played (used to decide when to show ads)
  • Your sparks balance and upgrade levels
  • Your daily reward progress (streak day, last claim date and any bonus waiting for your next game)
  • How many free-sparks ads you have watched today, and when (used for the daily limit)

This information is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes it from your device (see Cloud save for the copy kept in your Google Play Games account).

Google Play Games Services (optional)

If you use Google Play Games on your device, the Game may sign you in automatically, and you can also sign in from the Game. Google Play Games Services is provided by Google LLC. When you are signed in:

  • Google shares your Play Games player ID, gamer name and profile picture with the Game, so it can show you on the leaderboard. The Game does not receive your email address.
  • Leaderboard: your best score is sent to Google and shown on the Game's "Best Score" leaderboard, together with your gamer name and profile picture. Other players may be able to see it, depending on your Google Play Games profile settings.
  • Friends: if you choose to allow it, Google shares your Play Games friends list with the Game so it can show your friends' scores. You can refuse or change this at any time in your Google Play Games settings.
  • Cloud save: the Game saves a copy of your progress (best score, sparks balance, upgrade levels, unlocked items, selected style, tutorial status and daily reward progress) in your Google Play Games account. This lets you get your progress back on a new phone or after reinstalling. Your settings, ad counters and statistics stay on your device only.

This information is stored and processed by Google, not by us, under the Google Privacy Policy.

Inviting friends

The "Invite a friend" button opens your phone's normal share menu with a short message, your best score and a link to the Game on Google Play. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself, and we do not see who you share with.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, AdMob may automatically collect information from your device, including:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system and app version
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • Crash and performance information related to ads

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Google may use it to show you personalised or non-personalised ads, to measure ad performance and to prevent fraud. You can read how Google uses this information here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the Game's Settings screen.

Rewarded ads. Some ads are optional. You choose to watch them in exchange for an in-game reward, such as continuing a game, earning extra sparks or doubling a daily reward.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • Playing without signing in: you can play the whole Game without Google Play Games. Leaderboards and cloud save simply won't be used.
  • Google Play Games: you can manage your profile visibility, friends sharing and sign-in in the Google Play Games settings on your device.
  • Ad personalisation: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).
  • Consent: players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland can update their ad consent in the Game's Settings screen.
  • Deleting your data: uninstalling the Game deletes all game data saved on your device. To delete your leaderboard scores and cloud save, delete your Google Play Games data for the Game in your Google Play Games settings, or contact us and we will help.

If you are in South Africa, you have rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Users elsewhere may have similar rights under their local laws. Contact us to exercise them.

Security

We do not store your personal information on our own servers. Information collected by Google AdMob and Google Play Games Services is handled under Google's security practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
